# RealMart Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

RealMart is planning to establish a new supermarket branch in another city.
This project analyzes historical sales data from existing RealMart branches
to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance,
payment preferences, and branch-level performance.

The analysis was developed using Microsoft Power BI to transform raw
transactional data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to analyze existing RealMart supermarket
sales data and develop an interactive report that provides meaningful
business insights to support branch expansion planning.

The dataset contains information about:

- Customers
- Products
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Tax
- Sales
- Cost of goods sold
- Gross income
- Payment methods
- Customer ratings
- Branches
- Cities

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across cities and branches
- Analyze product-line performance
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze payment method preferences
- Compare customer segments
- Evaluate branch-level transaction performance
- Analyze gross income
- Provide business insights for future branch planning

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides an overview of:

- Total sales
- Total quantity
- Total transactions
- Average rating
- Gross income
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by product line
- Sales by city

### 2. Customer & Payment Analysis

Analyzes:

- Payment methods
- Customer types
- Gender-wise sales
- Product-line ratings

Interactive filters are available for:

- City
- Product Line
- Customer Type

### 3. Branch & Profitability Analysis

Analyzes:

- Sales by branch
- Gross income by branch
- Transactions by branch
- Sales by branch and product line

A branch slicer allows interactive branch-level analysis.

### 4. Business Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes:

- Branch performance
- Product performance
- Customer behavior
- Payment preferences
- Recommendations for future branch planning

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Branch C generated the highest sales and transaction volume among the existing branches.
- Food and beverages generated the highest sales among the product lines.
- Cash represented the largest share of sales by payment method.
- Mumbai recorded the highest sales among the cities in the dataset.
- Branch-level product mix provides useful information for evaluating future store inventory planning.

---
## 💡 Business Recommendations

The analysis can be used as a benchmark when planning a new RealMart branch.

Recommendations include:

- Use product performance across existing branches to inform inventory planning.
- Ensure availability of high-performing product categories.
- Support multiple payment methods.
- Consider customer membership programs to encourage repeat purchases.
- Track sales, transaction volume, and gross income as key performance indicators.
- Combine historical sales analysis with external information such as market size,
  competition, demographics, rental costs, and operating costs before selecting a
  new branch location.
